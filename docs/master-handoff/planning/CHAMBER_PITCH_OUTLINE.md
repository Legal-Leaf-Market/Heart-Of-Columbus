# Chamber / partner pitch outline

Working proposal, not an announcement of affiliation or a final commercial offer.

## Lead

**Athens of the Prairie: experience the city, support the people who make it work.**

Heart of Columbus proposes a new public doorway into local life: an illustrated, architecture-led city experience connected to a reliable business directory, stories, trails, Columbus Passports and participating merchant campaigns. Over time, those same records and relationships support a full operational platform for businesses and community organizations—a city-scale ERP.

## Demonstration order

1. Jacob's personal Back YOUR Blue tribute: why recognition and people matter.
2. The architectural artwork and a modest, honestly labeled spatial pilot.
3. One business record accessible consistently from map, search and world.
4. A passport and a useful landmark trail.
5. A draft pop-up/hunt campaign, showing what a participating owner can control.
6. The operational roadmap, evidence from CRH's network growth, and the multi-city model.

## What to ask for first

A problem-review conversation, introductions to willing merchants, feedback on a bounded pilot, and guidance on authoritative local records. Do not imply that the Chamber should endorse a finished city ERP today. Offer a specific review artifact and a measurable use case.

## Value hypotheses to test

- Visitors discover architecture and nearby independent businesses in one coherent journey.
- Residents find accurate information, events and community stories.
- Small businesses maintain one verified profile and gain useful campaign/operational tools.
- Partners can contribute authoritative information without surrendering all operational data.
- The platform can be replicated with each city's own identity and relationships.

These are hypotheses, not measured outcomes. The product currently has an existing public directory and completed artwork/research assets; the planned ERP, verified passport/rewards, merchant backend and custom 3D city are not complete.

## Partnership facts

Jacob reports ongoing communication with the Columbus, Bloomington and Nashville Chambers through Crossroads Wedding and Event Co. He and the LLC are not Chamber members. No endorsement, CRH partnership, city contract or official-government role is claimed. Bloomington and Nashville are the next intended city targets, followed by Franklin and Seymour.

## Founding and community programs

The intended first ten founding members receive free upgrades for life. Terms still need precise definition before sale. Religious organizations are intended to receive reduced rates with the same quality of support. Veteran-associated organizations are intended to have scholarship and recognition opportunities; eligibility, funding and amounts are not yet established.

## Close

Ask which narrow local problem deserves the first real pilot, who will steward its data, which organizations want to participate, and how success should be measured. Make the next step practical and reviewable while retaining the city-scale ambition.
