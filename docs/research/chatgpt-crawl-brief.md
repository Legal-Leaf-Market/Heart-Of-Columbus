# Things to crawl for Heart of Columbus

For ChatGPT (or Claude in Chrome). Written 2026-11-24 by scripts/chatgpt-crawl-brief.mjs; regenerate it, don’t hand edit.

Heart of Columbus is a local news and directory site for Columbus, Indiana. Its history section has short articles that need more facts, and some of their sources refuse automated reads from our build machine, especially The Republic (therepublic.com). You can read them. Please do.

## How to bring things back (important)

- **One block per article**, headed with the article’s slug, in the order below.
- For each page you read: the **canonical URL** as loaded (not a redirect or a tracking link), the **date you read it**, and the **facts it states, in your own words**, one per line.
- **Exact quotes** only when worth quoting, short (one or two sentences), in quotation marks, copied character for character, with who said it.
- **Never paste whole articles**, especially The Republic’s. We cite and link; the text is the publisher’s.
- If two sources disagree (a date, a number, a spelling), **say so** and give both.
- If a page has moved, give the new address. If it is gone, say so.
- Don’t add facts from memory or from pages you didn’t read. "Not found" is a fine answer.
- Skip national politics. We want Columbus history: places, people, companies, schools, churches, sports, events.
