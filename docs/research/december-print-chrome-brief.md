# Claude in Chrome: research for the December 2026 print edition

Heart of Columbus (heartofcolumbus.shop) is printing its first paper: 16 pages,
11 x 17 inch tabloid, a short run for friends and family. First drafts of the
news stories are in `content/print/2026-12/` in the repo, and the proof is at
https://heartofcolumbus.shop/print/2026-12/. Every fact a draft could not
confirm is marked `[CHECK: ...]` and shows yellow on the proof.

The build sandbox cannot read several of the sites below (The Republic,
WKKG and Indiana Capital Chronicle refuse automated requests). You can. Please
work through the tasks in order and return ONE markdown document in the format
at the bottom.

## Rules

- Facts only, each with the page you read it on and the date you read it.
- Do not copy whole articles. A short quote (one or two sentences) is fine
  where a story will cite it; say who said it and where it was printed.
- If a page does not say it, write "not found" and where you looked. Never
  fill a gap with a guess.
- Do not log in to anything, submit forms, buy anything or click affiliate
  or tracking links.
- No em dashes or en dashes in what you write back. Write ranges with "to".

## Task 1: The riverfront (front page, `river.md`)

The city's project is called "Our River... Our Riverfront": removing the
century old low head dam on the East Fork of the White River between the
Second and Third Street bridges, with a trail, overlooks and an amphitheater.

Look at The Republic (therepublic.com, search "riverfront" and "low head
dam", newest first, especially a story around November 18, 2026), the city's
page at columbus.in.gov (Columbus Redevelopment, riverfront), and the city's
Facebook if public.

Find:
1. Is the dam fully removed? When did the in river work finish?
2. What is finished now, and what waits for spring?
3. Is there a ribbon cutting or opening date?
4. The current total cost and where the money comes from (READI, tax
   increment financing, Next Level Trails, private). Our draft has $5.6
   million READI, about $11.58 million TIF, $1.72 million Next Level Trails,
   $105,000 private, about $17.3 million total. Confirm or correct each.
5. The contractor's name, if published.

## Task 3: The camping law (page 5, `camping.md`)

Our draft says Senate Bill 285 makes camping on public land a Class C
misdemeanor (up to 60 days and $500), that Gov. Mike Braun signed it this
spring, and that it took effect July 1. Read the enacted law on iga.in.gov
and coverage in Indiana Capital Chronicle, WFYI and The Republic.

Find:
1. Bill number and session year as enacted, signing date, effective date.
2. The penalty, and the warning and second encounter rules.
3. The "no open shelter beds within five miles" rule: is it in the enacted
   text? Quote the line.
4. The diversion program language.
5. Did the section survive that bars cities and counties from policies that
   discourage enforcement and lets residents, businesses or the attorney
   general sue? Quote it.
6. Columbus since July 1: from the city, Columbus Police, the Encampment
   Task Force, United Way of Bartholomew County, Brighter Days Housing,
   Horizon House or The Republic. How many warnings or citations, how many
   shelter beds exist, and what those groups say they need this winter.
   Who chairs or speaks for the task force?

## Task 6: Printing quotes for an 11 x 17 newspaper

The paper must be exactly 11 x 17 inches trimmed. No exceptions: it is a
newspaper, not a magazine. Newspaper Club (289 x 380 mm) and
printnewspaper.com (260 x 370 mm) do NOT make that size, so skip them unless
they now offer 11 x 17.

Find printers that print a true 11 x 17 inch newsprint tabloid, folded (not
stapled), 16 pages, and ship to Columbus, IN 47201. Look for:
- Local or regional: printers in Columbus, Seymour, Indianapolis,
  Bloomington and Louisville that print newspapers or tabloids. Find who
  prints The Republic and whether they take outside work.
- National online newspaper printers that list an 11 x 17 tabloid size.

For each printer, return:
- Name, city, website, phone or email for quotes
- Whether 11 x 17 trim is offered exactly
- Price for 100, 200, 500 and 1,000 copies of a 16 page paper, black and
  white inside with color on the front and back pages if offered, and all
  black and white; shipping to 47201
- Paper (newsprint weight), turnaround time, and whether a mid December
  delivery is possible if the PDF is sent by about December 5
- File requirements: PDF version, bleed, margins, color profile, image
  resolution
- Minimum order

The target is under $2 a copy at a few hundred copies. If a printer needs a
human to quote, write down exactly what to ask and who to ask; do not submit
quote forms.
