# First Christian Church

Proposed article: **The church that helped Columbus see modern architecture differently**

## Sourced starting points

- Eliel Saarinen designed the church, completed in 1942. Its restrained geometry uses buff brick and limestone, a prominent facade cross and a separate campanile.
  Source: https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/directory/first-christian-church/

- The visitor center identifies it as the city’s first contemporary building. Treat that as the local source’s historical characterization rather than an uncited global superlative.
  Source: https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/directory/first-christian-church/
