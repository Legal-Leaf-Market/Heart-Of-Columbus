# Bartholomew County Courthouse

Proposed article: **The courthouse Columbus still walks into**

## Sourced starting points

- The Indiana Historical Bureau identifies Isaac Hodgson’s Second Empire courthouse as the county’s fourth, completed in 1874, with red brick and white limestone trim above a rusticated blue-limestone foundation.
  Source: https://secure.in.gov/history/state-historical-markers/find-a-marker/bartholomew-county-courthouse/

- The same state marker records replacement of the original slate roof with standing-seam copper in 1953, major remodeling in 1968, interior restoration completed in 1998, and National Register listing in 1979.
  Source: https://secure.in.gov/history/state-historical-markers/find-a-marker/bartholomew-county-courthouse/
