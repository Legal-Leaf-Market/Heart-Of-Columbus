# Cleo Rogers Memorial Library and Large Arch

Proposed article: **The plaza where brick meets bronze**

## Sourced starting points

- The library is I. M. Pei’s 1969 brick building. Its geometric massing relates to nearby First Christian; brick, concrete and limestone, with a coffered concrete ceiling, form the architectural vocabulary.
  Source: https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/directory/bartholomew-county-public-library/

- Henry Moore’s Large Arch dates to 1971. The patinated-bronze sculpture on the plaza was a gift of J. Irwin and Xenia Miller. Its organic gateway contrasts with the surrounding rectilinear buildings.
  Source: https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/directory/large-arch/
