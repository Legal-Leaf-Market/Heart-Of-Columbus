# The LEX / former North Christian Church

Proposed article: **From sanctuary to shared space: the next life of North Christian**

## Sourced starting points

- Eero Saarinen’s church was completed in 1964 after his death; Roche Dinkeloo & Associates completed the project. Dan Kiley designed the landscape. The hexagonal sanctuary and contrasting low roof and tall spire are defining elements.
  Source: https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/directory/the-lex-formerly-north-christian-church/

- BCPL’s August 19, 2024 board minutes record successful removal of the cross from the spire. This establishes cross removal, not removal of the full spire.
  Source: https://static.libnet.info/frontend-images/pdfs/mybcpl/Board_Minutes/2024/8minutes.pdf

- The current visitor-center listing uses The LEX and reports the property’s 2024 transfer to BCPL.
  Source: https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/directory/the-lex-formerly-north-christian-church/
