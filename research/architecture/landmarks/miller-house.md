# Miller House and Garden

Proposed article: **One home, three designers, a complete world**

## Sourced starting points

- Newfields records the 1953 commission by J. Irwin and Xenia Simons Miller and credits Eero Saarinen, Alexander Girard and Dan Kiley. The design joins a flat-roofed stone-and-glass house, colorful textiles and geometric gardens.
  Source: https://discovernewfields.org/newfields/miller-house-and-garden

- The visitor center dates the completed design to 1957. Newfields describes the honey-locust allée and confirms guided-tour-only access, with tours beginning at the visitor center rather than direct arrival at the house.
  Source: https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/directory/miller-house-and-garden/
