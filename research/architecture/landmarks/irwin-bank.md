# Irwin Union Bank / Cummins Irwin Conference Center

Proposed article: **A bank designed to feel like a room in a garden**

## Sourced starting points

- Eero Saarinen designed the 1954 bank with landscape architect Dan Kiley. Its low glass-walled pavilion replaced the idea of a bank as a forbidding masonry institution with an open, visible interior.
  Source: https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/directory/cummins-inc-irwin-conference-center/

- The visitor-center account describes Kevin Roche’s 1973 glass arcade and additions, and explains the later transition to Cummins conference and hospitality use. It identifies nine opaque ceiling domes as light shades, not skylights.
  Source: https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/directory/cummins-inc-irwin-conference-center/
