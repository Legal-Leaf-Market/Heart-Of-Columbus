# Chaos I at The Commons

Proposed article: **The machine at the heart of The Commons**

## Sourced starting points

- The official local directory identifies Chaos I as Jean Tinguely’s steel-and-motor kinetic sculpture at The Commons, using wheels, gears and iron balls. It credits J. Irwin and Xenia Miller’s gift.
  Source: https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/directory/chaos-i/

- There is a date conflict: the directory lists 1974; the visitor center’s public-art feature describes completion in 1975. Treat these as unresolved event dates until fabrication, installation and dedication records are checked.
  Source: https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/blog/must-see-public-artwork/
