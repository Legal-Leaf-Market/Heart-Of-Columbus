Crossroads Wedding and Event Co. is based in Columbus and serves couples within about two hours of town: Indianapolis, Bloomington, Nashville, Louisville and Cincinnati. Its line is backyard to ballroom, and its promise is that it runs your day so you do not have to.

## What it does

A wedding DJ starts at $1,000 flat and covers ceremony sound, cocktail hour and the reception, with MC duties dialed up or down to taste. A live solo acoustic set is $500 flat: one performer, singer songwriter style, for a ceremony or cocktail hour. One hour is the sweet spot and two is the most, and with enough notice the performer learns up to three songs just for you.

Bar service puts licensed, experienced bartenders behind the bar at backyard and do it yourself venues with no bar staff of their own, at $250 for one bartender or $400 for two. The host provides the drinks; Crossroads pours them. It also offers day of coordination: calling cues, lining up the wedding party and keeping the run of show moving.

It also plays holiday parties and corporate events, DJ and sound at $1,500 flat. [CHECK: corporate offer as published.] Book a 30 minute intro call at crossroadsweddingco.shop.
