Amy's Closet sells donated clothing, and the way it works is simple. You drop off the clothes you no longer wear. The shop checks them, cleans them and puts the good ones on the rack. Someone finds a great piece at a fair price. And 10 percent of every sale goes to Love Chapel, a food pantry in Columbus.

## Three checks

Every piece is inspected three times before it reaches the rack, the shop says. Donations are sorted the day they arrive, and anything with stains, holes, odors or heavy wear is pulled. Then items are freshened up, and zippers, buttons, snaps and seams are tested. Last comes one more look under good light before anything is photographed, posted or hung. If it is not right, it does not go out.

## Donating

The shop asks donors to wash or dry clean everything first, check the pockets, make sure zippers and buttons work, keep shoes together in pairs and bag clothes by type. In season clothing sells faster. Belts, scarves, handbags and jewelry are welcome too. Items with stains or rips, anything that smells of smoke or mildew, used underwear and socks, and recalled children's items stay home. Not sure? The shop says to bring it anyway and let the three checks sort it out. Message the shop on Facebook to set up a drop off.

## Shopping

Amy's Closet is at 3928 25th Street, open Tuesday through Saturday, 11 AM to 7 PM. Call (812) 565-2197. The shop also sells online, and resellers and bulk buyers are welcome. [CHECK with Amy: the hours, and that the Love Chapel gift is 10 percent of every sale.]
