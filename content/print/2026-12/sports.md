The schools’ athletic calendars list these December varsity home dates. Check the host school’s calendar before heading out, since times can change.

## Basketball

**Columbus North girls.** Center Grove Dec. 3 at 7:30 PM, Trinity Lutheran Dec. 5 at 2:30 PM, and East Central Dec. 10 at 7:30 PM. North lists the Center Grove game at Memorial Gym. The gym’s story is on page 14.

**Columbus North boys.** Greenwood Dec. 12 and Columbus East Dec. 18, both at 7:30 PM.

**Columbus East girls.** Trinity Lutheran Dec. 1 and Columbus North Dec. 17, both at 7:30 PM.

**Columbus East boys.** Trinity Lutheran Dec. 4 and Bedford North Lawrence Dec. 11, both at 7:30 PM.

## Swimming and diving

North hosts Seymour Dec. 3 and East Dec. 15, both at 5:30 PM. East’s girls calendar lists home meets against Shelbyville Dec. 3 and Jennings County Dec. 7, both at 6 PM.

## Wrestling

North hosts Perry Meridian Dec. 2 at 6 PM and the Columbus North Invite Dec. 12 at 9 AM. East hosts North Dec. 16, the Columbus East Invitational Dec. 19 at 9 AM and Westfield Dec. 23 at 7 PM. The schools list different start times for the Dec. 16 dual, so check with East before heading out.

## The Indiana Sentinels

The Sentinels host the Watertown Wolves Dec. 11 and 12 at 7:30 PM at Hamilton Community Center & Ice Arena. Single-game tickets are available through the [team’s website](https://indianasentinels.hockeyshift.com/home).
