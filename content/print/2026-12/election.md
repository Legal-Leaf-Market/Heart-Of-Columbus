Bartholomew County voters went to the polls on November 3 with a long ballot: four county offices with two candidates each, a county commissioner, all four county council districts, three state house seats, a state senate seat, two seats in Congress and a renewal of the school district's operating referendum.

The BCSC operating referendum passed with 61.08 percent of the vote. The county's official report lists 12,915 yes votes and 8,228 no votes. Page 3 explains the renewal.

## The county races

Republican incumbent Carl H. Lienhoop won another term as District 2 county commissioner, beating Democrat Grant Hale by 3,038 votes.

Republicans won four other contested countywide offices. Debra K. Beck Settle won recorder by 2,705 votes, Sandy Beatty won treasurer by 2,745, Vickie E. Michael won auditor by 3,149 and Shari J. Lentz won circuit court clerk by 3,808.

County Council winners were Greg Patterson in District 1, Leah Beyer in District 2, Janae Garner-Kelley in District 3 and Barbara Hackman in District 4. Garner-Kelley, a Democrat, won by 390 votes. The other three winners are Republicans.

Republicans Chris Lane, Ginny Whipple, Lindsey Holden and Jonathan L. Rohde ran unopposed for sheriff, assessor, 9th Circuit prosecutor and Superior Court 2 judge, respectively.

## The legislature and Congress

Depending on where they live, county voters chose between Michelle Davis and Ross Thomas for State Senate District 41, and among three state house races: Ryan Lauer and Josh Burbrink in District 59, Jim Lucas, Chris Bowen and Tyler J. Henkle in District 69, and Jennifer Meltzer and Allen J. (A.J.) Miller in District 73.

Jefferson Shreve won the 6th Congressional District, which includes most of Bartholomew County. Erin Houchin won the 9th, which includes Jackson, Ohio and Wayne townships and parts of other townships. The table shows only votes cast in Bartholomew County.

## Where to check the numbers

The county reported 43.66 percent turnout, with 23,675 ballots cast. That includes 11,373 walk-in absentee ballots and 904 paper absentee ballots.
