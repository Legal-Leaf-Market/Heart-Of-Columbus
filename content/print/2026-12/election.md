Bartholomew County voters went to the polls on November 3 with a long ballot: four county offices with two candidates each, a county commissioner, all four county council districts, three state house seats, a state senate seat, two seats in Congress and a renewal of the school district's operating referendum.

The referendum passed. White River Broadcasting reported that it won 63 percent of the vote, renewing BCSC's existing rate for eight more years. Page 3 has what it means for your tax bill. [CHECK: the certified percentage.]

## The county races

Every county office on the ballot had a Republican and a Democrat, according to the county's official list of candidates. For county commissioner in District 2, incumbent Carl H. Lienhoop faced Grant Hale. [CHECK: confirm Lienhoop was the incumbent and name the winner from the certified results.]

County recorder was Debra K. Beck Settle against Nancy Merbitz. Treasurer was Sandy Beatty against Elizabeth Cleland. Auditor was Vickie E. Michael against Kimberly Wadholm. For clerk of the circuit court, Shari J. Lentz faced Quisha Jackson. [CHECK: winners and margins for each, from the certified canvass.]

All four county council districts were contested: Greg Patterson and Lisa Crane in District 1, Leah Beyer and Zack Patchett in District 2, Mark E. Gorbett and Janae Garner Kelley in District 3, and Barbara Hackman and Michelle E. Carr in District 4. [CHECK: winners.]

Three county offices had one candidate: Chris Lane for sheriff, Ginny Whipple for assessor and Lindsey Holden for prosecuting attorney of the 9th Circuit, all Republicans.

## The legislature and Congress

Depending on where they live, county voters chose between Michelle Davis and Ross Thomas for State Senate District 41, and among three state house races: Ryan Lauer and Josh Burbrink in District 59, Jim Lucas and Chris Bowen in District 69, and Jennifer Meltzer and Allen J. (A.J.) Miller in District 73.

In Congress, the 6th District race was Jefferson Shreve against Cynthia (Cinde) Wirth, and the 9th District had four candidates: Erin Houchin, Brad A. Meyer, Tonya L. Hudson and Floyd Michael Taylor. [CHECK: winners, and which parts of the county each district covers.]
