Every edition of this paper will follow one idea through the decades. December's is where Columbus gathers.

Some of these places were built for it: a theatre that got new sound in the Depression, churches that changed what this town thought a church could look like, a glass bank with no teller cages, a mall that was really a living room. Others became gathering places later, like a park built on land the river still floods, a city hall with a courtyard cut into its front for the whole town, and a downtown that filled with art for an exhibition last year. The flood of 2008 showed what coming together meant when the town needed it most.
