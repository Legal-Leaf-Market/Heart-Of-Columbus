Eli and I made up as friends recently. I greeted her with a, "life's short" and hug less than a week ago after running into her at her birthday party. Her cousins invited us for free, it was such a treat.

## Getting there

Easy drive from Columbus, and parking was not hard to figure out and inexpensive.

## The view from my seat

We were in section 219.

Had a great view of the court and action.

**Rating: 5 out of 5**

## Food and drink

We got chicken fingers, fries, and cotton candy.

Prices were reasonable.

**Rating: 5 out of 5**

## The people working the building

Staff pointed us the wrong direction first for Chick-fil-A, but then two lovely ladies corrected us on where the new location was.

**Rating: 5 out of 5**

## The show

They had an elementary choir sing the national anthem and a middle school aged dance group. Both were phenomenal.

The halftime was Tyler's Amazing Balancing Act. He balances a bunch of stuff on his nose. I give it a solid 4, weakest point of the night.

**Halftime: 4 out of 5**

**Rating: 5 out of 5**

## Worth the drive?

I was truly impressed.

Everyone should go, every single Hoosier and foreigner.

**Overall: 5 out of 5**
