Eli and I had just found our way back to friendship. Less than a week before the game, we ran into each other at her birthday party. I told her, “Life’s short,” and gave her a hug. Simple as that.

A few days later, her cousins invited us along to see the Pacers take on the Thunder. They had half season tickets, we had a free invitation, and Columbus was an easy drive from the arena. An old friend beside me and a whole night of basketball ahead? That’s a pretty great way to spend an evening.

## Getting there

The Pacers were coming home from New York after a 113 to 106 loss to the Knicks the night before. They were 5 and 12, carrying a four game losing streak into a matchup with the 10 and 7 Thunder. [Pacers at Knicks result](https://www.nba.com/game/ind-vs-nyk-0022600285)

Then Indiana came home and flipped the script.

The drive from Columbus was easy, and parking was simple to sort out without costing a fortune. We were in and ready for the night without any hassle.

## The view from my seat

We landed in section 219, with a terrific view of the court and all the action. It was easy to settle in, look around, and enjoy the whole scene.

**Rating: 5 out of 5**

## Food and drink

Chicken fingers, fries, and cotton candy: the all star lineup of a very good game night. The prices felt fair, and the cotton candy brought a little extra fun to the seats.

**Rating: 5 out of 5**

## The people working the building

We got pointed the wrong way for Chick-fil-A at first. It happens. Two lovely staff members got us turned around and showed us where the new location was. A quick detour, a little help, and we were back in business.

**Rating: 5 out of 5**

## The show

The entertainment started well before halftime. An elementary choir sang the national anthem, and a middle school dance group followed. Both were phenomenal.

The Pacemates were sharp. I’m a show choir alum, so clean lines and tight timing always stand out to me. They nailed both.

And then the game kept giving us reasons to lean forward. Jalen Slawson came off the bench with a pair of threes, eight points, and four rebounds in his first shift. He blocked Shai Gilgeous-Alexander at the rim, helping spark a fast break that ended with an Obi Toppin three. Next came a steal and a one handed alley oop to Toppin. That is a lively way to announce yourself. [Pacers game rewind](https://www.nba.com/pacers/news/rewind-pacers-thunder-261123)

Indiana built a 13 point lead in the third, but Oklahoma City kept coming. In the final five minutes, the Thunder got within one or two points five different times. Every time, the Pacers answered.

Pascal Siakam delivered the biggest answers. He hit an 18 foot jumper with just over a minute left, then drilled a three with 45 seconds to go after Gilgeous-Alexander answered with a step back. On Oklahoma City’s next chance, Kelly Oubre Jr. blocked Ajay Mitchell’s shot. Siakam grabbed the rebound, and Ivica Zubac finished with a dunk to seal it.

The Pacers won 125 to 120. Siakam and Oubre scored 20 apiece, Zubac added 14 points and 15 rebounds, and Gilgeous-Alexander led everyone with 38 points and seven assists. Indiana shot 40.5 percent from three, compared with Oklahoma City’s 35.1 percent. [Reuters game recap and stats](https://www.reuters.com/sports/basketball/pacers-fend-off-slumping-thunder-snap-4-game-slide--flm-2026-11-24/)

At halftime, Tyler’s Amazing Balancing Act took the floor. He balanced a bunch of things on his nose, which earned a solid four out of five from me. It was the one part of the night that didn’t quite hit the same high note as everything around it.

**Halftime: 4 out of 5**

**Rating: 5 out of 5**

## Worth the drive?

Absolutely. The Pacers came home as underdogs, stopped the skid, and held off one of the league’s toughest teams in a finish that stayed close until the final moments. The drive was easy, the view was great, the food was fairly priced, and the entertainment kept coming.

But the best part for me may have been getting to share it all with Eli again.

Every Hoosier should make the trip at least once. Visitors should put it on their list, too. Go with a friend. Bring someone you’ve missed. You never know what a simple “Life’s short” and a hug might set in motion.

**Overall: 5 out of 5**
