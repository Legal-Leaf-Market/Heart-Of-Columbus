# Riverfront nearly done: a reader's photos

A lead for a reporter, not a story. Nothing here prints until a person confirms it.

## What the post says

A public Facebook post with eight photos, credited to Randy Royer, said the
riverfront project "is getting close to being completed," praised Milestone
Contractors' work, and said the poster was glad to be part of the project.
Who wrote the post (Randy Royer or someone sharing his photos) is not clear
from what was relayed.

## What the photos show (described, not stored here)

- The new riverside trail along the East Fork, with wooden rails, limestone
  block terraces (seating steps) and new light poles; the red pylons of the
  Robert N. Stewart (Second Street) bridge in the background.
- Stairs and terraces climbing toward the Upland Columbus Pump House deck.
- A stone block carved with a year that appears to read 1938, set in a new
  planting bed.
- A trash can marked "Riverfront District" with the city's C logo.
- Fresh planting, a bench of limestone blocks, and equipment still working
  near the parking area.
