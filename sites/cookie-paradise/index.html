Quick wins in your Shopify

Five minutes each. Every fix shows up on Heart of Columbus automatically.

  • Photos: Pretzel Perfect and Coconut Dream have none, so they stay off our menu until they do. (Flamingo Sugar too, when it is back.)
  • Ingredients and allergens: Pretzel Perfect and Coconut Dream have no description. Peanut Butter Chocolate Chip lists ingredients but no allergen line; worth adding peanuts. Pandan, Pumpkin Spice and Oatmeal Raisin have no allergen line either.
  • Pickup details: your shipping policy says pickup is Saturdays at 3346 Cox Ln; your home and contact pages say the trailer at 3950 25th St, Tuesday to Saturday. One place, one answer.
  • Tidy up: older products list the vendor as “My Store”, and a few web addresses still use old names (black velvet, green eggs and ham, chocolate chunk).
