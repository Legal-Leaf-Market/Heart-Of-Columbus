Green to a fault

Not lean. Not mean. Actually green.

Every few years the corporate world finds a new word for cutting costs. This is not that. Before building a single arch, Rachael researched which balloons are best for the environment, and she has kept looking for ways to waste less ever since.

All natural latex

Premium natural latex balloons from Sempertex, not the bargain bin kind.

Minimal plastics

Far less plastic than the average party store balloon, and the plastic there is gets salvaged.

Reuse everything

If a piece can go again, it goes again.

Least waste breakdown

When the party is over, it all comes down and goes out the best way possible.

Straight talk: natural latex is not the same as compostable, and we will not pretend it is. It is a long way past the average consumer balloon, and it keeps getting better.
