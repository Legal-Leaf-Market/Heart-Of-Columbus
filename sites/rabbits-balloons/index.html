Whimsy with a work ethic

How a project runs.

The look is otherworldly. The process is not. You know what you are getting, what it costs and when it shows up.

Step one

Tell me the vibe

Date, venue, colors and the feeling you want people to walk into. A photo, a mood board or three words all work.

Step two

Get a design and a quote

A sketch of the install and a clear price before a single balloon is ordered.

Step three

Setup, on time

Installed and finished before your guests (or your CEO) arrive.

Step four

Breakdown, low waste

When it is over, it comes down. Everything that can be reused is reused, and the rest goes out the least wasteful way possible.

Green to a fault

Not lean. Not mean. Actually green.

Every few years the corporate world finds a new word for cutting costs. This is not that. Before building a single arch, Rachael researched which balloons are best for the environment, and she keeps looking for ways to waste less.

All natural latex

Premium natural latex balloons from Sempertex, not the bargain bin kind.

Minimal plastics

Far less plastic than the average party store balloon, and the plastic there is gets salvaged.

Reuse everything

If a piece can go again, it goes again.

Least waste breakdown

When the party is over, it all comes down and goes out the best way possible.

1 in 3

That is how many cheap store balloons used to pop on Rachael while she built. With Sempertex she can build a whole arch without losing one. Fewer pops means less waste and a cleaner install.

Straight talk: natural latex is not the same as compostable, and we will not pretend it is. It is a long way past the average consumer balloon.
