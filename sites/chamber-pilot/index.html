What Heart of Columbus is

The visitor center, the local directory, order pickup and online booking for our town, in one place, plus a newspaper. Every Book and Order button goes straight to the business’s own booking or ordering page, so the business keeps the customer and every dollar.

Two roles that fit together

The Chamber

  • Speaks for Columbus business
  • Advocacy, events, connections, the network
  • Membership that opens doors

Heart of Columbus

  • The shop window: where residents and visitors find local businesses
  • The toolbox: websites, booking and back office software for owners
  • The paper: local stories, online and in print

One never takes the place of the other. Our message to owners is to be in both.

The ask

Help us run a pilot in Columbus:
  1. Introductions. Help us reach the first ten Founding Members among Chamber members, starting with the barbershops.
  2. Honest feedback. Tell us what Chamber members ask for that nobody gives them today, and where this falls short.
  3. Your name on it, if you want it. A pilot presented with the Columbus Area Chamber, on terms you are comfortable taking to your board.

What we bring back: the results, the numbers and the lessons, to the Chamber first. A pilot that works here is how we offer it to other chambers and cities.

Not only a website: a printed paper

We are going old school too. Heart of Columbus will publish a physical newspaper, hand delivered to neighbors 55 and up, so the people who built this town are not left behind by the screen.

Local stories, events, the history of Columbus decade by decade, and the businesses that make it home. Every story is written and checked by people, with its sources listed.

Things to decide together
