# TJ’s ChopShop — identity v1
Creative direction: a neighborhood institution, getting its next chapter. Sign painter lettering, a razor underline, warm paper and oxblood. Confident, welcoming, unpretentious.

## Identity
Primary mark: logos/logo-primary.svg. Transparent actual vector paths traced from the generated original, not an embedded bitmap. Original raster retained. Traced edges retain subtle raster irregularity; inspect enlarged before physical signage. No font dependency in logo. Ivory version for dark fields. Charcoal version for one-color production. Monogram for avatars and favicons only. Do not replace the wordmark with a stock font. Keep clear space equal to the height of the small location line on every edge. Primary mark minimum recommended width 220px; use monogram under that. Never stretch, rotate, recolor arbitrarily, add drop shadows, or place over busy photos.

## Palette
Oxblood #831D2C; ivory #F4EBDD; charcoal #211F1C; brass #BC9471; paper #E6DAC9. Ivory/charcoal or ivory/oxblood for body text and controls. Brass is decorative, not small body copy. Backgrounds warm and flat; avoid black gradients.

## Typography
Wordmark is artwork. Website: Georgia bold for expressive editorial headings; Arial for clean body and controls. System stacks require no webfont download. Headings 42–80px desktop, 36–48px mobile; body 17–19px, line height 1.55. Avoid making every heading uppercase. Keep readable text as HTML.

## Voice
Short, direct, human. Suggested creative line: “Your next cut. Your neighborhood shop.” Website line: “Pick a time. Sit down. Look sharp.” Do not claim decades in business, a founding year, services, awards or guarantees without TJ’s approval.

## Artwork and photography
The hero illustration is conceptual brand art, NOT a photograph of TJ’s premises. Label it as illustration where confusion is possible. Use real approved shop and haircut photos for before/after, team and results. Do not present generated people, premises or haircuts as actual customer work. Keep illustration to hero and accent surfaces. Patterns sparingly in dividers or packaging, not behind paragraphs.

## Applications
Header: compact logo, 160–220px, link to top. Hero: HTML heading on quiet space, illustration right. Booking: oxblood CTA with ivory text. Footer: ivory logo on charcoal. Apparel: one-color ivory logo on charcoal/oxblood shirt. Window: one-color ivory vinyl; obtain printer proof. Print: SVG masters are RGB; printer must supply CMYK conversion, bleed and material requirements. Do not claim these are press-ready PDFs.
