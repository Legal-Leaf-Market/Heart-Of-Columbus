Hey, I'm Deidra!

A little wild, a whole lot of heart.

I'm a small-town girl from Indiana with a big love for adventure. I've been behind the camera since 2010, and this little business of mine has grown right along with me. When I'm not shooting, I'm a mama to one pretty awesome little boy, chasing the outdoors, good food, loud music and road trips.

My style is fun and straightforward: the in-between moments, the real interactions and the real emotions. This is a collaboration. I'm not here to be a service provider, I'm here to make art with you.

Yes, I'll tell you what to do with your hands.Deidra, on posing
